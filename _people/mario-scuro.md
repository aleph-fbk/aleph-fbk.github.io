Mario Scuro is a Researcher in the Applied Cryptography (ALEPH) research unit at the Center for Cybersecurity, Fondazione Bruno Kessler. He holds a BSc in Mathematics from the University of Padua and an MSc in Mathematics (Cryptography specialization) from the University of Trento.
 
His research focuses on applied cryptography, spanning practical implementations and performance evaluations. He is currently working on zero-knowledge proofs and their applications in domains such as e-voting, machine learning, and digital identity.
